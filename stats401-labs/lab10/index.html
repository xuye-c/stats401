2. How to use voice

Click Start Voice Control and allow the microphone. It stays on while you talk, and the Heard line updates as words arrive. The command runs after you pause for about two seconds, or when you click Stop Listening. Speech recognition needs Chrome or Edge, and a page opened on localhost or HTTPS.

  • Show Asia zooms to a continent or area. Show Japan zooms to that country.
  • Highlight Japan outlines a country and keeps its neighbors visible.
  • Show increase, Show decrease, or Show unchanged dims every other status.
  • Zoom out moves up one level. Reset returns to the world and clears the filter.

4. Design reflection

This page extends the squarify treemap from Lab 6. The hierarchy is still continent, area, and country. Rectangle area still encodes GDP in billions of U.S. dollars, and color still encodes Increase, Unchanged, or Decrease. The novel input is voice, through the browser Web Speech API. “Show Asia” zooms to a region, “Highlight Japan” selects a country and opens its details, and “Show increase” filters by status. Reset and zoom out are spoken as well. Voice fits this treemap because many countries are thin slivers that are hard to click, while their names are easy to say, so navigation works more like search. The same functions stay on buttons and a typed command, and the microphone starts only after Start Voice Control. Recognition can confuse “South Africa” with “South America,” and it misses short names in a noisy room. The heard line and the action line make those mistakes visible.
